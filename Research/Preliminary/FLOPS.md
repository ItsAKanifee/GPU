## Summary 

### How do Floating Points Work
- Floating points split into 3 parts
    - Sign Bit:
        - 1 bit
        - Marks number as negaative (1) or positive (0) 
    - Exponent:
        - 8 or 11 bits (32/64 bit respectively)
        - Contain exponent of 2 to multiply given hex value by
    - Mantissa:
        - 23 or 52 bits (32/64 bit respectively)
        - Numbers after the decimal point before exponent is applied
