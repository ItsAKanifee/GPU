# Integer Core Experiments

This folder collects experiments related to the integer-core datapath: ALU operations, fixed-point arithmetic, and low-level datapath design.

## Objective
Prototype and validate integer datapath components (adders, multipliers, shifters) and verify integration with control and pipeline stages.

## Hypothesis
Simple ripple-carry or carry-lookahead designs with modest pipelining will meet the target frequency and area for integer operations and provide a baseline to compare against floating-point units.
