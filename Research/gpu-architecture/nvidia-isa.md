## Notes

This is the Nvidia ISA and serves a simmlar purpose as the AMD ISA in providing deeper architectural understanding of how the GPU works. This is a lot less dense than AMD's ISA, and thus, there is a lot more information left out in comparison to AMD, but it does provide a good understanding of what is going on a higher level than AMD
